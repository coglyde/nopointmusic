## Silver titanium body + yellow gold hardware (current direction)

Brushed titanium body, warm yellow gold hardware — a two-tone luxury watch language. Think Royal Oak Two-Tone, Rolex Day-Date in steel + yellow gold, vintage McIntosh amplifier gold-on-silver. The titanium reads minimalist and architectural; the gold lifts it to luxurious. Restrained, not flashy.

The arm is also yellow gold — see `arm.md` for the matching prompt.

Attach `vinyl.png` as the lighting reference.

Aspect: square-ish (about 1.2 to 1), reads more like a sculpture pedestal than a hi-fi component.

```

Absolutely not chrome, not mirror-polished stainless, not raw aluminum (no cold blue cast).
